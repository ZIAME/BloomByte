## Description
BloomByte is a cozy pixel-art focus timer that turns your work sessions into a garden of butterflies.

Pick what you want to focus on, set your session length and press start. A little caterpillar grows through seven hand-drawn pixel stages while you work, from munching a leaf to spinning a chrysalis. Finish the session and your butterfly hatches. End early and it stays a chrysalis, so every butterfly is one you really earned.

Use it your way
• As a normal app window you can resize and maximize
• As a floating widget that stays on top in the corner of your screen
• As a tiny corner pill with just your timer and caterpillar while you work

Focus sessions
• 25, 50, 90 minute or custom sessions, with short breaks in between
• Choose how many sessions to run and see how long the whole set will take
• Brain dump: park stray thoughts mid-session without losing focus
• A gentle chime and notification when a session or break ends

Butterfly journal
• Day, week and month views of every butterfly you've grown
• See your goal, how it went and your caption for each session
• Spot your best days at a glance

Notes
• Quick notes that save as you type
• Search and sort, with undo when you delete
• Turn any note into your next focus goal

Little touches
• Greets you by name, morning to night
• Soft 8-bit sound effects you can switch off
• Everything stays on your computer. No account, no ads, no tracking.

Every illustration in BloomByte is hand-drawn pixel art.
