# BloomByte Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 20 November 2026_

BloomByte is a focus timer and notes app made by Ziana. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it.

## What BloomByte stores

- The name you enter when you first open the app
- Your focus sessions (goal, length, how it went, your caption, brain-dump thoughts)
- Your notes
- Your settings (timer lengths, sound on/off, window size and position)

## Where it is stored

Everything is saved **only on your own computer**, in the app's data folder. BloomByte has no account system and no server. It does not send your information anywhere, and it does not include analytics, tracking or ads.

## Sharing

Your information is never sold, shared or transferred to anyone, because it never leaves your device.

## Deleting your data

Uninstalling BloomByte removes the app. Your data stays in its folder on your computer until you delete it, so you can reinstall without losing anything. If you installed from the Microsoft Store, Windows removes the app's data when you uninstall.

## Children

BloomByte doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new date.
